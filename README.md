## 🎮 Games & How to Play

### 🕵️ 1. Movie Imposter (3–10+ Players)

1. **Setup**:
   - Choose your categories (**Tollywood**, **Bollywood**, **Hollywood**, **Shows**, **K-Drama**, **Cartoons**, or any combination).
   - **👥 Player Groups**: Save your regular game groups (e.g. "Family", "Weekend Crew", "Roommates") directly in local storage. Switch groups with 1 tap, rename them, or update current player names anytime.
   - **↕️ Drag & Drop Reordering**: Arrange players in any order using the unidirectional drag handle (`↕`).
   - **🔥 Spice It Up**:
     - **Number of Imposters**: Scale up imposters for larger groups (e.g. 2 imposters for 6+ players).
     - **Discussion Timer**: Set a timer from 1 to 10 minutes, or `0 min (No Limit)`. The phone provides physical haptic vibration as soon as the timer hits `00:00`!
     - **Give hint to Imposter**: Toggle whether the imposter receives a subtle trivia clue (lead actor, director, genre, channel, country of origin, hint, etc.).
     - **Reveal Name to Imposter**: Optional modifier where the imposter knows the secret title, turning the game into a battle of pure deduction and bluffing!

2. **Secret Role Deal**:
   - The device is passed to each player in a **randomized dealing order** so no player is always first.
   - Each player holds their card to secretly reveal either the **Title** or their **Imposter Role**.
   - If "Reveal Name to Imposter" is active, the imposter sees the title highlighted with a flame orange **TITLE** badge.
   - Release the hold before passing the phone to ensure complete privacy.

3. **Discussion & Accusation**:
   - Tap **Start Discussion** to spotlight a **randomly chosen player** to start the round!
   - Players discuss the movie/show/cartoon and ask questions without leaking the title directly.
   - The imposter tries to blend in using their subtle clue or knowledge of the title!
   - **🔄 Next Round**: If players cannot identify the imposter, tap **Next Round** to draw a fresh unplayed movie from the selected categories while keeping the imposter secret for another round of clues!
   - Vote on who the imposter is, then tap **Reveal Imposter** to see if the group won.

### 🪢 2. Hangman Battle (2 Teams • New in v1.4.0)

A pass-and-play duel featuring strike tracking, optional trivia hints, and competitive session scoring!

1. **Team Setup**:
   - Customize Team A and Team B names (e.g., `Team Popcorn` vs `Team Cinema`).
   - View cumulative session scores preserved across all rounds.
2. **Word Entry (Word Creator Spotlight)**:
   - The setting team is spotlighted on a high-contrast dark card.
   - Enter any secret word, movie title, or phrase (spaces and punctuation reveal automatically).
   - Add up to 3 optional clues (e.g., *"Directed by Christopher Nolan"*, *"2014 Sci-Fi masterpiece"*).
   - Tap **Lock Word & Pass Phone →** to hand the device to the guessing team.
3. **Guessing Arena**:
   - Guess letters or digits using the typewriter keypad.
   - **7-Strike Tracker**: Incorrect guesses strike through the letters `H - A - N - G - M - A - N` one by one.
   - **Clues Drawer**: Open hints anytime—viewing clues adjusts the win reward from `+2 Pts` to `+1 Pt`.
4. **Scoring Rules & Next Round**:
   - `• Guess without clues: +2 Points`
   - `• Guess with clues opened: +1 Point`
   - `• 7 strikes hit: Setting team +1 Pt`
   - Tap **Swap Roles & Next Round →** to alternate setting/guessing turns while continuing the session score!
